Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how Bus Breeze (the "Game"), a mobile puzzle game developed by Fatih ("we", "us"), handles information. The Game does not require an account and we do not run any servers that collect your data. The Game is free to play and is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob and by optional in-app purchases through Google Play.

1. Information stored only on your device

To work, the Game saves the following only on your device. It is never sent to us:

  • Game progress: level, coins, lives, boosters, daily reward streak and piggy bank.
  • Settings: sound and vibration.
  • Codes of completed purchases, used so that the same purchase is never credited twice.
  • When ads were last shown, so ads are not shown too often.

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the Game's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the Game. If Android backup is on, Android may include this data in your Google account backup; this is managed by Google and you can turn it off in your device settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob): a banner, full-screen ads between levels, optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for a reward, and an ad when you return to the Game. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / app set ID),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (for example, ads viewed or tapped) and diagnostic data.

Depending on your choices and where you live, ads may be personalised based on this information. Google's use of data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): the first time you open the Game, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your choice about personalised ads. You can change it any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the Game.
  • US state privacy laws: where applicable, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information through the same privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • No ads: the optional "No Ads" purchase removes banners, ads between levels and the ad shown when returning to the Game. Rewarded videos stay available only if you choose to watch them.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

The Game offers optional coin packs, bundles, "No Ads" and similar items. All payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play gives the Game only the product ID and a purchase token, which the Game uses on your device to deliver the item. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google processes payment information.

5. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help.

6. Security and retention

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the Game. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own policies.

7. Your rights

Under laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK or US state privacy laws, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold personal data about you on our side, most requests concern Google; you can manage them through the privacy options in the Game, your Android settings and your Google account. For any question, contact us.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date.
